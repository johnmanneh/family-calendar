Overview

When ("the app") is a family calendar application built to help families organise events, tasks, and schedules together. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By using When, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and password (stored as a secure hash)
  • Family data — names and relationship labels of family members you add to your group
  • Calendar events — titles, dates, times, locations, descriptions, and privacy settings you create
  • Tasks — task titles, due dates, assignments, and status updates
  • Profile photo — if you choose to upload one
  • Device push notification token — to deliver event and task notifications to your device

Voice Input

When includes an optional voice command feature powered by your device's on-device speech recognition. Voice audio is processed locally on your device and is not sent to our servers. We do not store, record, or transmit your voice.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the family calendar service
  • To share events and tasks with the family members you invite
  • To send you push notifications about events and tasks (only if you enable them)
  • To restore your session securely across devices

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Within your family group — events and tasks you create are visible to the family members you have invited, according to the privacy settings you choose
  • Push notification services — Apple APNs (iOS) and Google FCM (Android) are used to deliver notifications; only your device token is shared, not your personal data
  • Legal requirements — if required by law

Privacy Circles

When includes a privacy circle system. Events marked as private are never shared in full — family members outside your inner circle see only that you are busy, not the event details. You are always in control of who sees what.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on a secured server. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We use industry-standard practices to protect your information.

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data is removed from our systems.

Children's Privacy

When is designed for family use and may be used by families with children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 independently — children use the app as part of a family group managed by a parent or guardian.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the address below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
