Delete Your Account

You have the right to delete your it's for us account and all associated data at any time. To request deletion, send an email to the address below and we will process your request within 30 days.

Request Account Deletion

What Gets Deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your account information (name, email address, password)
  • Your profile photo
  • All calendar events you created
  • All tasks you created or were assigned
  • Your family group membership and relationships
  • Your push notification token
  • All messages and notifications associated with your account

Retention Period

Once your deletion request is received, your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days. This action is irreversible.

Partial Data Deletion

If you would like to delete specific data without deleting your entire account — for example, removing certain events or family members — you can do this directly within the app. If you need assistance, contact us at the address below.
