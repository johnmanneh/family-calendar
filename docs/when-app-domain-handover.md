## Faith-Anchored Domain Concepts

Both the When logo (hanging strings — Ecclesiastes 3:1 "There is a time for everything") and the Worn Word logo (cross from T+L — John 14:6 "I am the Way, the Truth, and the Life") carry the same thread: **God's path and appointed timing over a life.** The domain should reflect that.

### The Strongest Concept — KAIROS

In the Bible there are two Greek words for time:
- **Chronos** — clock time, sequential, human-measured
- **Kairos** — God's appointed time, the right moment, divine timing

Ecclesiastes 3:1 is kairos language. John 14:6 is the path through those appointed moments.
